### Services

Q: What services does AmeriCare offer?
A: AmeriCare offers a comprehensive range of in-home healthcare services including:
- Skilled Nursing Care
- Personal Care Assistance
- Companionship Services
- Specialty Care (Alzheimer's, Dementia, Parkinson's, ALS)
- Pediatric Nursing
- Pediatric Rehabilitation
- Hospice and Palliative Care
- Medicaid Programs (CCSP/SOURCE and GAPP)

Q: What is skilled nursing care?
A: Our Skilled Nursing services provide the highest level of medical care within the comfort of your home. Our team of registered nurses and licensed practical nurses provide medication management, wound care, disease management, and post-surgical support.

Q: What is personal care assistance?
A: Our Personal Care Assistance helps with activities of daily living (ADLs) such as bathing, dressing, grooming, meal preparation, and mobility support. We tailor care to promote independence and enhance overall well-being.

Q: What is companionship care?
A: Companionship at AmeriCare means meaningful human connection and practical assistance to combat loneliness. Our caregivers are friendly faces, listeners, and companions — through conversation, games, or shared meals. We help your loved ones remain active and engaged in life.

Q: What specialty care does AmeriCare provide?
A: Our Specialty Care services are designed for clients with unique healthcare needs. Our caregivers are trained to support individuals with Alzheimer's, dementia, Parkinson's, and ALS. We create personalized care plans ensuring safety, comfort, and quality of life.

Q: Does AmeriCare provide pediatric care?
A: Yes. Our skilled pediatric nurses are specially trained to provide medical care, administer medications, and monitor the health of children with medical needs. We also offer physical, occupational, and speech therapy services designed to help children reach their developmental milestones.

Q: Does AmeriCare offer hospice and palliative care?
A: Yes. We offer end-of-life care services that prioritize comfort, pain management, and emotional support for both the patient and their family.

Q: Does AmeriCare accept Medicaid?
A: Yes. AmeriCare participates in Georgia Medicaid waiver programs including CCSP/SOURCE and GAPP.

Q: What is the CCSP/SOURCE program?
A: CCSP/SOURCE (Community Care Services Program / Service Options Using Resources in a Community Environment) are Georgia Medicaid waiver programs designed to assist elderly and disabled individuals. They offer in-home support services to help avoid long-term care facilities, with benefits including personalized care, comprehensive support, and a wide range of services including personal support and adult day health.

Q: What is the GAPP program?
A: GAPP (Georgia Pediatric Program) provides in-home nursing services for medically fragile children under 21. It ensures children with complex medical needs receive continuous, high-quality care in their homes, with benefits including skilled nursing, care coordination, and family support.

Q: What types of service can I choose from?
A: You can choose from the following types of service:
- Companionship
- Personal Care
- Skilled Nursing
- Specialty Care
- Other (custom needs)
